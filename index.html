This tool provides estimates only. It is not financial, legal, or tax advice — consult a qualified professional before making a real buy-or-rent decision.

Assumes a fixed-rate, fully amortizing mortgage. Each month, whichever option costs less — owning or renting — has its savings invested at the alternative-return rate; the down payment and any closing costs are invested from month zero in the renting scenario. Home equity is shown net of estimated selling costs, as if the property were sold at that point.
